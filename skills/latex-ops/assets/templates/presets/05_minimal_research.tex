\begin{abstract}
A sober academic-paper reference optimized for reading, scanning, equations, tables,
figures, and conventional scholarly structure.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The hierarchy is deliberately small. The paper should make arguments, evidence, equations,
tables, and figures easy to find without adding graphic noise.

\section{Method}
Use semantic mathematical environments:
\begin{equation}
y = \alpha + \beta x + \varepsilon.
\end{equation}

\section{Results}

\section{Conclusion}
A research preset should remain subordinate to venue requirements. When a journal or
conference controls typography and layout, preserve that class instead.
