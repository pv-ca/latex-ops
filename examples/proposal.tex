\section{Summary}

Eight members of the operations team need to run supplier calls, write clear incident
e-mails and present weekly figures in English. We propose a 12-week course with two
90-minute sessions a week at your office, built around the team's real tasks rather
than a general textbook.

\begin{keybox}{What the team will be able to do by week 12}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item Lead a 20-minute supplier call: agenda, clarification, agreed next steps.
  \item Write an incident report e-mail that a manager can act on without follow-up questions.
  \item Present a weekly KPI slide and answer questions about it.
\end{itemize}
\end{keybox}

\section{How the course runs}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\sffamily\bfseries}p{.17\linewidth}X@{}}
Weeks 1--2 & Individual needs interview and a short placement task; group plan agreed with you. \\\addlinespace
Weeks 3--10 & Three four-week blocks: calls, written reports, presenting figures. Each block ends with a task based on real work. \\\addlinespace
Weeks 11--12 & Consolidation and a final simulation: a supplier call and a KPI presentation, observed and given written feedback. \\
\end{tabularx}

Progress is reported to the operations manager after weeks 4, 8 and 12, with attendance,
completed tasks and one concrete recommendation per participant.
